Aleksey Porvin

Poet & Essayist

Aleksey Porvin (b. 1982) is a Russian poet, essayist, and literary critic, widely recognized as one of the most remarkable and consequential voices in contemporary literature.

At the heart of Porvin's poetic achievement lies a singular synthesis of the European poetic tradition and the most daring techniques of the avant-garde.

Aleksey Porvin's poetry has secured a place of singular importance in the modern literary landscape, marking him as a writer of rare intellectual and artistic stature.
